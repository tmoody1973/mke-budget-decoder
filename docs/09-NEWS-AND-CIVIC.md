## Rules

1. **Numbers come from the database, never from articles.** A topic card shows our cited figures; an article is shown as headline, outlet, date and link only (no excerpts).
2. **Coverage is listed evenly** by outlet, with no editorial comment about any outlet.
3. **Civic dates are not budget figures.** They cite their own source (the Council's release); legal deadlines cite the budget PDF (Summary front matter, p.4).
4. **Claim labels are neutral** — Matches the budget / Rounded / Not in the budget documents / Different measure — never "wrong", and each label is reviewed by Tarik before it goes live (same flow as the glossary).
5. Articles are added by hand in a reviewed data file, not scraped automatically.
